How It Works

From car details to price in 4 steps

  1. 01

    Enter Car Details

    You fill in the form: car name, year, kilometers, fuel type and the other details.

  2. 02

    Data Preprocessing

    The details are turned into numbers. Categories like "Diesel" become 0/1 columns (one-hot encoding).

  3. 03

    Machine Learning Prediction

    The Linear Regression model multiplies each value by a weight it learned from past car sales.

  4. 04

    Estimated Price

    The result is shown as an estimated selling price in rupees.
